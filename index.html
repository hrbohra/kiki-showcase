Kiki · a working build

Your mutual friend, built.

A working version of Kiki on Kiki’s stack: React Native for iOS and web, NestJS and Postgres behind it. Invite-only sign-in, places sorted by how close the host is to you in people, live messaging, and an introduction written by an AI that can only say what the connection graph can prove.

Built end to end by Harsh Bohra for the founding full-stack engineer role.
hrbohra.vercel.app · harshrbohra@gmail.com

Open the app · 30 seconds Why it’s built this way › The code › Four decisions

Not a booking app. A way of proving you are worth letting in.

The design job was to show who vouched for you, what it costs them, and what you owe back. Sorting by degrees of separation instead of price is the whole product; every screen below holds it up.

01 Native-first Expo One codebase, native iOS gestures, safe-area insets honoured on every screen.
02 A pure domain layer Degrees, vouches and standing are computed once and unit-tested; every screen reads the same answer.
03 A bespoke map Web-Mercator over real listing coordinates rather than a dropped-in map SDK.
04 Honesty in the copy “Some will say no. That’s the system working.” Nothing softened to sell.

For the actual native iOS app: download the repo, start it with Expo on a computer, then scan the QR it prints to open it in Expo Go on your phone. This is the only way to feel the finer work — haptic taps as a match forms, native spring animations, gesture physics and true on-device timing. (Full details in the repo README.)
