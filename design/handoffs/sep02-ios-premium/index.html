What I left out of your review, on purpose. Gyroscope parallax, 3D metal tier badges, holographic borders, film grain, confetti, NFC bump and neon “club mode” are all on the explicitly-avoid list in your own PLAN_IOS.md — and they are the tells of a demo trying to look expensive. Premium here means weight, timing and restraint: things that make the product feel certain rather than decorated.
