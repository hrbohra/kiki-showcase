1. The signature motif: the Loop

Two overlapping rings — the pretzel/knot mark reduced to its simplest legible form: two circles that share their middle. It is the one shape that means "two people, one overlap", which is the entire product.

Where it appears, and at what strength
Map surface — stroke #17A589 at 10% opacity, 150×90, bottom-left, purely as texture.
Listing photo placeholder — stroke #FFFFFF at 35%, bleeding off the bottom-right corner, so every "photo" carries the mark.
Profile hero — stroke #17A589 at 9%, 240 wide, bleeding off the top behind the avatar.
Trust banners (listing preview, guest-book quote card) — a 16px solid-stroke Loop as the leading glyph instead of an emoji.
Never at more than 18% opacity behind content, and never more than one instance per screen.
Why: a motif that only ever appears as texture cannot compete with content, but it accumulates — by the fourth screen the shape reads as "Kiki" without a single logo lockup. Build: one <Svg> component, <Circle cx=38 cy=30 r=21 /> ×2 in a 100×60 viewBox, stroke only. Requires react-native-svg.

7. Order of work

1. Tokens (§2) — one file, unblocks everything else.

3. ListingCard + MapCanvas — the first screen anyone sees in the demo.
